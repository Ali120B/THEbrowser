# UX principles

1. **Work is spatial.** Browsing objects keep their position and relationship.
2. **Reality over resemblance.** A card is a real Chromium surface or explicitly unavailable.
3. **Calm density.** Essential navigation remains close; advanced actions appear on demand.
4. **Durable by default.** Workspaces survive restart, display changes, and recoverable failure.
5. **Direct manipulation, complete keyboard parity.** Every pointer operation has an accessible command.
6. **Honest privacy and performance.** State and protection indicators describe what is actually active.
7. **Original implementation.** Stack-inspired interaction principles do not copy Stack assets or branding.
